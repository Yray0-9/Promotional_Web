The Problem with Plastic Sponges

Why Filipino Families Deserve Better

Most households use cheap plastic sponges that create environmental and practical problems:

  • Break apart quickly and leave plastic pieces on dishes
  • Clog drains with microplastics
  • Don't last long – need replacement every month
  • Contribute to unnecessary plastic waste
  • Not strong enough for stubborn stains

There has to be a better way.
